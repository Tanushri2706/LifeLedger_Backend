
## 🚧 Development Status

LifeLedger is currently under active development.

We have completed the initial product planning and technical architecture, including:

* Product Requirements Document (PRD)
* System Architecture

* Development Roadmap

* Next.js
* React
* TypeScript

* HeroUI

* Aceternity UI
* Framer Motion

**Backend**

* Node.js
* Express.js
* TypeScript
* MongoDB Atlas
* Mongoose
* JWT Authentication

### Core Product Modules

1. Personal Health Dashboard
2. AI Health Document Analysis
3. Digital Health Vault
4. Health Timeline
5. Nearby Hospitals & Doctors
6. Doctor Appointments
7. Family Health Management
8. Emergency Health Profile

### Current Focus

> Building the foundation first — architecture, design system, reusable components, security, and scalable APIs before implementing advanced AI healthcare features.

### Project Philosophy

LifeLedger is designed around four principles:

**Privacy · Trust · Simplicity · Intelligence**

The goal is not to overwhelm users with healthcare data, but to transform their medical information into a clear and understandable personal health experience.

---

### ⚠️ Important Medical Disclaimer

LifeLedger's AI features are intended to assist users in understanding their health information. AI-generated insights are **not medical diagnoses** and should not replace professional medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
